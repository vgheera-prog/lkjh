Royal Culinary Tradition

Our Mughal Gastronomy

Located on the historic Grand Trunk Road in Singhpura Lahore, Lahore Shahi Qila Restaurant revives the legendary tastes of the royal Mughal courts. Each handi is slow-cooked over low flame in authentic earthen pots, absorbing the rustic aromas of unrefined spices and rich desi butter.

Our Karahis are forged on roaring flames in traditional cast iron woks using ripe country tomatoes, hand-pounded ginger, and fresh green chillies without artificial flavoring or compromises.

Live Charcoal BBQ

Marinated for 12 hours in secret royal spices and flame-broiled on coal embers.

Earthen Handis

Traditional clay vessels that lock in the deep natural juices and aromas.

Clay Tandoor

Hot, blistered naans and rotis fresh from our burning clay oven.

Swift Hot Delivery

Packaged in thermal insulated boxes to arrive piping hot at your table.
